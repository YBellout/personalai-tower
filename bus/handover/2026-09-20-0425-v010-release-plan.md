**Four facts verified while writing it, not assumed.** They change the shape of S-002
and S-003, so they are recorded here as well as in the plan:
- The shell that reaches the hub is a Linux VM on the MacBook with the hub mounted,
  not macOS. git 2.34.1 is present in it and github.com answers 200 over HTTPS.
- `security` does not exist there, so the macOS Keychain cannot be read by whatever
  does the nightly push. The rule "no secrets in files, Keychain only" cannot be
  satisfied as written, and that is now the central question of S-003a rather than
  something discovered at 2am on night one.
- `launchctl` and `osascript` do not exist there either, so the nightly trigger
  cannot be launchd and cannot be a .command launcher. It has to be the scheduled
  task the launch prompt already asks for.
- Nothing runs while the Mac is asleep or the desktop app is closed. Listed in the
  plan as the first of four ways M0 can fail for the wrong reason.
