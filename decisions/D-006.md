# D-006  Folder grants for the stray scan

    Scope:       control-tower
    State:       answered
    Decided by:  Yassir, 2026-09-20
    Supersedes:  nothing

## Context

Residency means every governed file is under the root. Proving that needs the Tower to
look where files usually stray, and it holds a grant to none of those places.

## Options

1. **Recommended, and taken.** Grant `~/Desktop`, `~/Documents`, `~/Downloads`.
   `~/Developer` is dropped from `config.yml`: it does not exist on this Mac.
2. Grant the whole home folder. Not grantable, and more than a residency check needs.
3. No grants. Residency stays unverifiable for every project outside the root, which
   today is three of five.

## Reversibility

Free. A grant is per session and lapses when the session ends.

## Cost of not deciding

`audit.py` at M1 would report residency as unknown for three projects forever.

## How it is applied

Grants do not outlive a session, so they are requested when the scan first runs, at
M1, not today. Requesting them now would open three folders for a scan that does not
exist yet.
