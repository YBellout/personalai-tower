# D-014  The link prompt is pasted at adoption step 8, and says so

    Scope:       global
    State:       answered
    Decided by:  Yassir, 2026-09-20
    Supersedes:  nothing. Amends the block delivered by S-005.

## Context

On 2026-09-20 the link prompt was pasted into all four ungoverned projects. Every one
of them asked questions instead of working, because the block points each chat at a
registry entry, a policy file, an evolution map and an orders folder, and for all four
projects three of those four do not exist.

The block was written for adoption step 8. Steps 1 to 7 create what it points at.
Nothing in the block said so, so nothing stopped it being pasted at step zero.

## Options

1. **Recommended, and taken.** Say it, in two places. A precondition in the how-to
   prose, and a hard clause inside the block itself: if your registry entry says
   `unmanaged`, or your policy file, evolution map or orders folder is missing, name
   what is missing and stop. Do not ask, do not propose, do not write, do not
   improvise the missing files.
2. Precondition in the prose only. Cheaper, and the prose is not what the receiving
   chat reads. The chat reads the block, so the block is where the stop has to live.
3. Change nothing and rely on the order being followed. That is what was relied on
   today, and it cost four chats.

## Carried with the pick, and reversible on one word

The block now also opens with the D-007 marker line, `[GENERATED PROMPT |
<project-id> | 2026-09-20 | execute as written]`. It was the only prompt the Tower
produces for Yassir to paste that did not carry it, while `OPEN_SESSION_PROMPT.md` and
`LAUNCH_PROMPT_v0.1.0.md` both do. That is a D-007 conformance gap in the Tower's own
deliverable and self-governance does not allow it to stand. The counter-argument is
real: custom instructions are standing rules rather than a prompt executed once, so
"execute as written" reads oddly there. Say the word and it comes out.

## Reversibility

Both edits are text in one file plus the Tower's own copy of it, and they cost a
re-paste into every linked project. **Today that cost is zero**, because all four
chats are being closed and no project is linked. This is the cheapest moment this
change will ever have, which is the whole reason it is being made now rather than
after M0.

## Cost of not deciding

The same paste happens again at the next project, and the failure repeats with the
chats that matter, the ones holding real files.
