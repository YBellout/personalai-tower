# D-011  Design system starting scope

## Context

The apps must read as one product family rather than independent websites. Only one
interface has been iterated on and accepted, the Virtual Closet's, and that project
is ungoverned and scheduled last on purpose.

## Options

1. **Recommended, and taken.** Extract from the Virtual Closet UI. Tokens only in
   release one, then card grid, filter sidebar and status pill, then the Portal shell.
   Sequencing caveat carried with the pick: extracting tokens is read-only and can
   happen at any time, but no app may take the design system as a dependency until
   the Closet is governed, or the shared look has an ungoverned source of truth.
2. Design it fresh, independent of any app. No ungoverned source, and it discards the
   one interface that has already been accepted.
3. Wait for the Portal. Correct ordering on paper, and the Portal is M6, so every app
   built before then improvises.

## Reversibility

Tokens are the cheapest thing in the system to change and the most expensive thing to
change late: while nothing depends on them, a rename is a find and replace, and once
three apps import them it is a coordinated release. The caveat above is what keeps
the reversible window open.

## Cost of not deciding

Each app picks its own colours and spacing, and the family look becomes a retrofit
rather than a default.
