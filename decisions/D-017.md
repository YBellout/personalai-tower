# D-017  Unattended jobs enter the hub through one granted runner app

    Scope:       global
    State:       answered
    Decided by:  Yassir, 2026-09-20
    Supersedes:  nothing

## Context

The hub lives in ~/Documents, which macOS privacy protection (TCC) guards. The M0
diagnostic, bus/reports/heartbeat-diagnostic-2026-09-20-234628.txt, shows launchd ran
the heartbeat 22 times on schedule and every automatic run failed, exit 126, "Operation
not permitted": the bash launchd starts may not open a file in ~/Documents. Every future
unattended job, mirror, audit, night promote, hits the same wall.

## Options

1. **Recommended, and taken.** One small runner app is the single entry point for
   every unattended job. Yassir grants it Documents access once. Nothing else on the
   Mac gets broader access. Least privilege.
2. Full Disk Access for /bin/bash. Works in one toggle, and gives every bash process
   started by anything on the Mac access to everything TCC protects. Rejected.
3. Move the hub out of ~/Documents. No grant needed, and it moves PersonalShopper's
   4 GB of live work with its launchers, and strips the hub of TCC's protection.
   Rejected.

## Reversibility

High. The runner is a thin shim regenerated by a launcher, the grant is one row in
System Settings, and the launchd plist is one file. Option 2 remains the fallback,
narrowed to Full Disk Access for the runner app alone, never for bash.

## Cost of not deciding

M0 cannot pass: its exit test is three days of automatic runs and none can reach the hub.

## Applies to

The Tower first, and every governed project's unattended Operations (D-016) from
adoption: they are dispatched by the Tower's runner, never scheduled on their own.
