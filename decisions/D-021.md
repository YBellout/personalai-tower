## Rejected, and why

- Closing X-001 while a front door still ran from dev/: leaves exactly the unrecorded
  exception policy/self-governance.md calls a breach.
- Widening the runner's standing grant for a nightly stray scan: more coverage, but a
  wider permission held at all times, against D-017's least privilege.

## Reversibility

All high. Every answer is a line in a design not yet built. Answer 14 costs two clicks
at S-021 and can be swapped for narrowing X-001 instead until the moment the app is
rebuilt.

## Closes when

Each answer is built and tested within its story. None is built before S-004 passes.
