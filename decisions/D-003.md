# D-003  Local mirror destination

    Scope:       global
    State:       answered, one fact outstanding
    Decided by:  Yassir, 2026-09-20
    Supersedes:  nothing

## Context

The design puts a local mirror before any offsite copy, and the frozen copy at adoption
step 3 is the first thing it has to protect. The mirror needs a second place on disk,
outside the root, that is not a cloud-synced folder.

## Options

1. **Recommended, and taken.** An external drive if one exists. Otherwise
   `~/PersonalAI-mirror` on the internal disk, as an honest half-measure: it survives a
   bad promote or a deleted folder, it does not survive the disk.
2. Internal disk only, permanently. Cheapest, and the day the SSD fails the hub and
   its mirror go together.
3. Offsite only. Skips the fast local restore that is the whole point of mirroring
   first.

## Reversibility

Free. It is one path in `config.yml`. Moving the target later is one full re-sync.

## Cost of not deciding

Adoption step 3, the frozen copy, has nowhere to go, so no project can be adopted.

## Outstanding

Whether an external drive exists. Until Yassir says, `config.yml` holds the fallback,
`~/PersonalAI-mirror`. **Nothing is being mirrored today**: `mirror.sh` is M1.
