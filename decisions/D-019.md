# D-019  How promote.sh behaves at its three edges

    Scope:       global. promote.sh is the one promote path for every governed project.
    State:       answered
    Decided by:  Yassir, 2026-09-21, as three short control answers in the Evolutions
                 chat, transcribed here by the control room. Claude proposed, Yassir picked.
    Supersedes:  nothing. Settles the three open items of S-016's design.

## Context

S-016 designs promote.sh and rollback.sh. Three edge cases had no answer in any existing
decision: a project with no test suite yet, what happens to old prod/ snapshots, and a
project with no smoke check.

## Options and picks

1. **Empty `health.tests`.**
   Picked: promote proceeds to the smoke check instead of refusing outright.
   Rejected: refuse until a suite exists, which would block every project's first
   promote, the Tower's included.
   Cost accepted: S-016's C1, "promote refuses when a test fails", guards nothing for a
   project until it has a suite.
2. **Archived prod/ snapshots beyond five.**
   Picked: pruning the oldest past five is the rotation's own byproduct, not a deletion
   of project files under policy/self-governance.md, so no derogation is needed.
   Rejected: move each pruned snapshot to _to_delete/, which would grow without end.
   Boundary: this reading covers promote's own rotated snapshots and nothing else. Any
   other removal still goes to _to_delete/.
3. **Empty `health.smoke`.**
   Picked: promote refuses, with a named reason, rather than skipping verification.
   Rejected: promote without a smoke check.
   Consequence: the Tower's own first promote, S-021, needs dev/bin/smoke.sh to exist
   first.

## Reversibility

All three are lines in promote.sh and one clause of reading on self-governance. Changing
any of them is a small story and touches no data.

## Cost of not deciding

S-016 could not be built as designed, and M1's exit test depends on it.

## Closes when

S-016 is built and its criteria close with these three behaviours tested.
